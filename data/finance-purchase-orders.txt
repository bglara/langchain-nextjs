Purchase Order Authority

Under $500: hiring manager can approve in the PO tool (Coupa).

$500–$5,000: department director.

Above $5,000: VP of the function, plus Finance controller.

Split POs to dodge a threshold are a code-of-conduct violation. Software subscriptions over 12 months count at full TCV, not monthly.

Atlas demo units for sales come from SKU ATLAS-X7-HW with reason code DEMO; they still need a PO.
