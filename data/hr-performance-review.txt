Performance Review Cadence

Nimbus runs two formal review cycles: March (calibration 1–15 March, letters by 31 March) and September (same shape). Ratings are 1–5. A 5 requires written examples and director sign-off.

Compensation changes from the March cycle take effect on the 1 May payroll. September is developmental only — no merit increase.

Employees with a rating of 2 enter a 60-day performance plan. A second consecutive 2 is grounds for separation.

Skip-levels with the skip manager happen in the off-cycle months (June and December), 30 minutes, not scored.
