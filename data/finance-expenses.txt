Expense Reports (Concur)

Submit in Concur within 30 days of spend. Receipts required above $25. Missing receipts over $25 are denied, not exceptioned by Finance.

Alcohol: not reimbursed, except a single drink at a documented customer dinner, max $20, named attendees in the memo.

The $850 stipend uses code STIPEND-850. Travel uses Navan; do not double-submit hotel from Navan and Concur.

Managers approve within 5 business days or the report escalates to Finance ops.
