Nimbus Robotics Employee Handbook

Nimbus Robotics is a robotics company founded in 2019, specializing in warehouse automation drones.

Vacation policy: Full-time employees at Nimbus Robotics receive 27 paid vacation days per year, plus 6 company holidays. Unused vacation days roll over up to a maximum of 10 days into the following year.

Remote work policy: Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements require approval from a department director.

Equipment stipend: New employees receive a one-time equipment stipend of $850 to set up their home office.

Parental leave: Nimbus Robotics offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for all new parents, regardless of gender or path to parenthood.
