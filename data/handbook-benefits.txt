Nimbus Robotics Benefits FAQ

Vacation time at Nimbus is generous: full-time staff get 27 vacation days a year. Up to 10 unused days carry over; anything above that is forfeited. Company holidays are extra and do not count against the 27.

Health insurance: Nimbus pays 80% of the premium for employees and 50% for dependents. Enrollment is during the first 30 days of employment or at the annual open-enrollment window in November.

The equipment stipend ($850, one-time, for a home office) is mentioned in the employee handbook as well. It cannot be combined with a second monitor request from IT — pick one.
