Nimbus Parental Leave Guide

Nimbus offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for every new parent, including adoption and surrogacy. Leave may start up to 2 weeks before the due date or placement date.

To start leave, file form PL-16 in Workday and notify People Ops at least 30 days in advance when the date is foreseeable.

Health insurance continues on the same 80%/50% split during leave. PTO does not accrue during parental leave but existing balances are preserved.

A gradual return (3 days/week for the first 4 weeks back) is available on request and does not consume additional leave.
