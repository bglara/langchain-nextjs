Git Workflow

Trunk-based development on main. Feature branches, squash merge, one approving review required. Direct pushes to main are blocked.

CI must be green: unit, lint, and the Atlas sim smoke (except docs-only diffs labeled skip-sim).

Secrets never in git. Use Infisical. A leaked Groq or LangSmith key is a SEV2 and rotates within the hour.

Release tags: atlas-firmware-x.y.z and magpie-x.y.z. Do not reuse a tag.
