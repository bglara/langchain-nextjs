Corporate VPN (WireGuard)

Nimbus uses WireGuard. Config is pushed by Jamf; do not download random .conf files.

Split tunnel: only 10.0.0.0/8 and staging.nimbus.internal go through the VPN. General web traffic does not.

Personal devices cannot join Nimbus-Corp or the VPN. Guest WiFi is the only option for phones without MDM.

A lost laptop: lock in Jamf, then treat as a security incident if the disk was unlocked. Report in #sec-incidents.
