Business Travel Policy

Flights under 4 hours: economy only. Flights of 4 hours or more may be premium economy; business class needs a department director pre-approval in Concur.

Per diem is $75/day domestic and $95/day international, no receipts required for meals under per diem. Alcohol is not covered except one drink at a customer dinner (see finance-expenses).

Hotel cap: $220/night in SF/NYC/Seattle, $160 elsewhere, before taxes. Ride-share to/from airports is allowed; rental cars need manager approval.

Book through Navan (formerly the Nimbus travel desk). Out-of-policy bookings can be rejected at reimbursement.
