Magpie Picker — Product Spec

The Magpie Picker is a floor AMR (autonomous mobile robot) for case-pick aisles. Throughput: about 800 picks per hour in a standard 12-meter aisle with human-in-the-loop exception handling.

Magpie does not fly. It must not be confused with the Atlas X7 drone. Mixed Atlas+Magpie sites use Cloud Sync as the shared telemetry bus.

Safety: Magpie stops if a human enters its 1.5 m LIDAR keep-out. ISO 3691-4 certified.

Software subscription is required for aisle maps; a disconnected Magpie will idle after 15 minutes.
