Nimbus 401(k) — Harbor Nest Retirement

Plan administrator: Harbor Nest Retirement. Employee deferrals can start on the first payroll after 30 days of employment.

Nimbus matches 100% of the first 4% of eligible pay. Vesting of the match is a 2-year cliff: leave before 24 months and the match is forfeited; employee deferrals are always yours.

Loans against the 401(k) are not allowed. Hardship withdrawals follow IRS rules and require People Ops + Harbor Nest approval.

Default investment is the Harbor Nest Target 2055 fund unless the employee picks another option in the portal.
