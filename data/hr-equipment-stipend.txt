Home Office Equipment Stipend

New hires receive a one-time $850 stipend. Submit receipts in Concur within 60 days of start date. After day 60 the stipend expires and cannot be reinstated.

Eligible: monitor, dock, chair, keyboard, headset, webcam. Not eligible: phones, standing desks over $600, or a second monitor if IT already issued one.

The stipend cannot be combined with an IT second-monitor ticket. Choose one path and note it on the Concur report with code STIPEND-850.
