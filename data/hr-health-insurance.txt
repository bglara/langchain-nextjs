Nimbus Health Insurance — Helios Health PPO

Plan name: Helios Health PPO (carrier Helios). Nimbus pays 80% of the employee premium and 50% of dependent premiums.

In-network deductible: $750 individual / $1,500 family. Out-of-pocket max: $4,000 / $8,000.

Enrollment window: first 30 days of employment, or annual open enrollment 1–30 November, coverage starting 1 January.

Dental (BrightSmile) and vision (OptiClear) are separate and 100% employee-paid. FSA contribution max follows IRS limits; Nimbus does not offer an HSA with this PPO.
