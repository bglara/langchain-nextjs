Nimbus Remote Work Policy (detail)

Default hybrid: up to 3 remote days per week. Core collaboration hours are 11:00–15:00 Pacific Time on remote days; Slack green-status is expected then.

Fully remote roles require a department director plus People Ops sign-off. Fully remote employees must visit HQ at least once per quarter (travel booked under the travel policy).

Remote days are not an entitlement during on-call primary weeks or the 5-day onboarding bootcamp.

Home-office internet is not reimbursed; the $850 equipment stipend is the only home-office cash benefit.
