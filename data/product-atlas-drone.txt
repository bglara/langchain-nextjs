Atlas X7 Warehouse Drone — Product Spec

The Atlas X7 is Nimbus's flagship indoor warehouse drone. Payload: 12 kg. Typical flight time: 40 minutes with a standard pack, 28 minutes at max payload.

Navigation uses ceiling UWB beacons plus downward optical flow. It is not rated for outdoor flight or for people-moving.

Fleet telemetry ships to Nimbus Cloud Sync. Minimum firmware for Cloud Sync pairing is Atlas 4.2.1. Do not skip 4.1.x when upgrading from 4.0.

A spare battery cycle is rated 500 charges. Replace packs that report under 80% health in the Atlas Bay app.
