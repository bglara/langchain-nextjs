Nimbus Robotics Office Guide

The Nimbus Robotics headquarters is located on the 4th floor of the Beacon Tower building.

Guest WiFi: The guest WiFi network is named "Nimbus-Guest" and the password is "drone-flight-42".

Parking: Visitors can park for free in Lot C for up to 3 hours; after that, validation is required at the front desk.

Office hours: The office is staffed from 8am to 7pm on weekdays. Badge access is required outside of those hours.

Kitchen: The 4th floor kitchen restocks snacks every Tuesday and Friday morning.
