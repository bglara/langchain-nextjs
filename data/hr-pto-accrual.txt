Nimbus Robotics PTO Accrual

Full-time employees accrue vacation at 2.25 days per calendar month, which totals the 27 days listed in the employee handbook. Accrual pauses during unpaid leave.

Part-time staff (under 30 hours/week) accrue at 1.0 day per month, capped at 12 days per year.

PTO requests of 5 or more consecutive days must be submitted at least 14 days in advance via Workday. Same-week requests can be denied by the manager.

The PTO calendar year runs 1 January through 31 December. Carry-over is capped at 10 days; unused days above that expire on 31 December and are not paid out except at termination in California.
