Employee Referral Bonus

Engineering referrals (software, firmware, robotics, ML): $3,000 paid after the new hire's 90th day.

All other functions: $1,500 on the same 90-day rule.

The referrer must be listed on the Greenhouse application before the first interview. Retroactive tagging is not paid.

Contractors and People Ops recruiters are not eligible. The bonus is taxable and paid on the next payroll after day 90.
