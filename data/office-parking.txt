Parking at Beacon Tower

Visitors: Lot C, free for 3 hours, then front-desk validation. Overnight visitor parking is not allowed.

Employees: Lot B with a hanging permit. The permit is issued on Day 1 of bootcamp.

EV chargers: stalls 7 through 12 in Lot B, 4-hour limit, Nimbus badge required to start a session.

Lost permits: $40 replacement via Facilities ticket FAC-PARK. Do not park in the loading dock; Magpie crates unload there weekdays 7:00–9:00.
